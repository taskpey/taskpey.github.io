Summary

  • TaskPey is a task platform. Members complete small promotion jobs for advertisers and partners (follow, like, subscribe, join) and receive a reward for every approved task.
  • The membership fee is a one-time access fee that opens your member account. It is not an investment, deposit or saving, and no profit or return is promised.
  • Earnings are not guaranteed. They depend on how many tasks are available and how many you complete correctly.
  • A tax is deducted from withdrawals (the rate is shown in the app before you confirm), and every request is checked manually.
  • Fake or edited screenshots, multiple accounts or misuse of bonuses can lead to rewards being removed and the account being closed.

1. What TaskPey is

TaskPey is a task platform. Advertisers and partners post small promotion jobs, for example following an account, liking a post or video, subscribing to a channel, watching a video or joining a group. Members complete the job, send proof, and receive a reward in their TaskPey wallet once the proof is approved.

TaskPey is not a bank, an investment company, a savings scheme or a financial adviser.

2. Membership fee

  • To open the member area (tasks, wallet and withdrawals) you pay a one-time membership fee. The amount is shown on the activation screen in US dollars with the Pakistani rupee amount below it.
  • The fee pays for access to the platform. It is not an investment, a deposit, a loan or a share, and it earns no interest, profit or return.
  • Payments are checked manually by the team. Your membership starts after the payment is approved.
  • Send the exact amount to the number shown on the activation screen and enter your own sender number and Transaction ID (TID) correctly. If a payment is wrong or missing, contact support (see section 10).

3. Earnings are not guaranteed

  • TaskPey does not promise any fixed daily, weekly or monthly income.
  • What you can earn depends on the tasks that are available. Many tasks have a limited number of spots and end once the limit is reached.
  • Rewards are paid only for tasks that are completed correctly and approved. Incomplete, fake or edited proofs are rejected.
  • Please do not join expecting guaranteed profit, and never pay the membership fee with money you cannot afford to lose.

4. Wallet and currency

  • Your wallet is shown in US dollars. The conversion uses a rate set by TaskPey, which may be changed from time to time.
  • Payments in and out are made in Pakistani rupees through the payment methods shown in the app (for example Easypaisa or JazzCash).

5. Withdrawals

  • You can request a withdrawal when your balance reaches the minimum amount shown in the app.
  • A withdrawal tax / service charge is deducted from each withdrawal. The rate, the amount deducted and the amount you will receive are shown before you confirm.
  • Requests are reviewed manually, so they are not instant. Make sure your account name and number are correct. TaskPey is not responsible for money sent to wrong details that you entered.
  • TaskPey may delay or refuse a withdrawal that looks fraudulent until it has been checked.

6. Referrals and signup bonus

  • A referral code from an existing member is required to create an account.
  • The referral commission is paid once, when the invited friend's membership is approved. The amount is shown in the app and may be changed.
  • New accounts may receive a signup bonus, shown in the app. The bonus can be changed or switched off at any time.
  • Self-referral, fake or duplicate accounts and any other misuse can lead to bonuses and commissions being removed.

7. Rules for members

  • One person, one account. Use your real name and a working email address.
  • Do your tasks yourself. Do not use bots, fake accounts, edited screenshots or copied proofs.
  • Follow the rules of the platforms where tasks are done (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, X, Telegram, WhatsApp, Google and others).
  • Be polite with support and other members.
  • If these rules are broken, TaskPey may reject proofs, reverse rewards and bonuses, or suspend or close the account.

8. Your data

To run the service we store your email address, name, country, optional profile photo, wallet and withdrawal history, proof screenshots and support chats. This data is kept in Google Firebase, is used only to operate and protect TaskPey, and is not sold. The admin team can see it to review payments, tasks and withdrawals. Ask support if you want your account and data removed.

9. Other companies

TaskPey is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, X, Telegram, WhatsApp, Google or any other platform. Their names and logos belong to their owners and are used only to show which app a task is about.
